The earlier M1.2 annotation transcribed Excadrill's ability as `拔沙`. Reinspection of the enlarged screenshot shows `拨沙`, matching the PP-OCRv6 result and the canonical Sand Rush entry (`pokeapi:ability:146`). The corrected fixture annotation is in `tests/fixtures/m12_ground_truth.json`; this correction changes the prior one-ability-miss interpretation in the M1.2 report. These are development fixtures, so the perfect fixture score does not establish accuracy on unseen layouts.

The observed team is Salamence, Tyranitar, Corviknight, Excadrill, male Indeedee, and Sneasler. Species are stored separately from form. The icon matcher supports male Indeedee form `pokeapi:form:876`; for the other five, the screenshot does not yield a distinct canonical form ID, so `form_id` remains null. Five members have 13 of 14 required fields observed; Indeedee has 14 of 14. The team remains **incomplete** because none of the 84 required field instances has been explicitly reviewed and marked `manual_verified`. No active cross-screen conflicts were found in the two screenshots.

Remaining unknown or unsupported information: five form IDs; all six levels, natures, EV spreads, and IV spreads. The screen shows small allocation numbers and nature direction arrows; allocation numbers are preserved as displayed values in separate fields, while the arrows are not decoded into nature names. A null form ID means no form-specific identity was established, rather than proof that no alternate form exists. Manual review can confirm an unnumbered base form without inventing a PokeAPI form number.

Reproduce the extraction and checks from the project root:

```sh
.venv/bin/python main.py --images tests/fixtures/team_configuration_moves.png tests/fixtures/team_configuration_stats.png --ocr paddle-v6
.venv/bin/python main.py --team-summary
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_team_configuration.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
```

The test compares each visible ability, item, ordered move, and actual stat against manually annotated screenshot regions. It also checks duplicate-species isolation, species/slot conflict handling, preservation of conflicting evidence, manual correction, base-form verification, and the six-member completion gate.
