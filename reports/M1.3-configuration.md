Both images show all six cards in the same order. Source SHA-256 hashes, page identifiers, timestamps of processing, text, pixel regions, confidence, and canonical resolution methods are retained in the JSON. The screenshots are the only configuration evidence here; they do not display levels or numeric EVs/IVs.

The earlier M1.2 annotation transcribed Excadrill's ability as `拔沙`. Reinspection of the enlarged screenshot shows `拨沙`, matching the PP-OCRv6 result and the canonical Sand Rush entry (`pokeapi:ability:146`). The corrected fixture annotation is in `tests/fixtures/m12_ground_truth.json`; this correction changes the prior one-ability-miss interpretation in the M1.2 report. These are development fixtures, so the perfect fixture score does not establish accuracy on unseen layouts.

The observed team is Salamence, Tyranitar, Corviknight, Excadrill, Indeedee, and Sneasler. Male Indeedee is `pokeapi:pokemon:876`. The icon and text together distinguish the ordinary variants of Salamence, Tyranitar, Excadrill, and Sneasler. Corviknight resolves to `pokeapi:pokemon:823` from its exact species name and the M-C scoped identity index: PokéAPI's only other row is Gigantamax Corviknight, which is outside the listed M-C battle mechanics. Its thumbnail remains visually ambiguous, so the identity is marked `knowledge_resolved`, not visually verified. All six members have 14 of 14 required fields populated; Corviknight's variant is rule-inferred rather than visually observed. The team remains **incomplete** because none of the 84 required field instances has been explicitly reviewed and marked `manual_verified`. No active cross-screen conflicts were found in the two screenshots.

Remaining unknown or unsupported information: all six levels, natures, EV spreads, and IV spreads. The screen shows small allocation numbers and nature direction arrows; allocation numbers are preserved as displayed values in separate fields, while the arrows are not decoded into nature names. All six null form IDs accompany resolved Pokémon IDs and are valid: no extra form ID is needed to distinguish these variants. The M-C exclusion of G-Max is scoped to current mechanics and does not assert that the PokéAPI form or a future Champions mechanic is impossible.

Reproduce the extraction and checks from the project root:

```sh
.venv/bin/python main.py --images tests/fixtures/team_configuration_moves.png tests/fixtures/team_configuration_stats.png --ocr paddle-v6
.venv/bin/python main.py --team-summary
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_team_configuration.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
```

The test compares each visible ability, item, ordered move, and actual stat against manually annotated screenshot regions. It also checks duplicate-species isolation, species/slot conflict handling, preservation of conflicting evidence, manual correction, base-form verification, and the six-member completion gate.
