# M2.3.3-A: chronological evidence fusion and partial replay admission

Date: 2026-10-15. Implemented using existing cached observations only. Sampling, frame extraction, OCR models, glyph thresholds, parser grammar, and message deduplication remain unchanged. M2.3.3-B, M3.1, and Bayesian inference were not started. No commit or push was performed.

## Result

The concrete HUD gaps are repaired in the new automatic replay: Corviknight occupies battle 0’s observed right slot at 364.011667s; Armarouge occupies battle 4’s right slot at 432.073333s with 117/162 HP and subsequently 66/162; battle 1’s left-slot Salamence retains one continuous instance through 172/172, 106/172, and 52/172; battle 2’s early Froslass and Rillaboom HUD observations are retained before Politoed appears.

Across all five recordings, 220 active-slot HUD observations are examined: 198 identities admitted, 20 deferred, and two conflicting. Absolute HP is applied from 110 observations; 85 percentage observations retain percentage units. Twenty-five move-revelation observations establish 19 distinct instance/move pairs while applying **zero move effects from those announcements**. These are evidence-processing counts, not recognition accuracy or complete battle coverage.

## Implementation and evidence policy

- [tracking/evidence_fusion.py](../tracking/evidence_fusion.py) implements `hud_observation` transitions, identity evidence checks, prospective message context, and move-only admission. [battle_events/schema.py](../battle_events/schema.py) adds the observation type; it is not a synthetic switch, send-out, or faint.
- [tracking/state_engine.py](../tracking/state_engine.py) invokes fusion only for HUD observations and messages explicitly marked `fusion_context`. Existing events and rule profiles retain their prior path. Initial state at recording time zero is unknown; HUD and message evidence enter in timestamp order, with HUD before messages from the same instant. Observation time is not asserted to be action onset.
- HUD events carry raw species/icon/HP fields, their confidence and resolution metadata, source timestamp, frame hash, image path, and battle ownership. The transition maintains accepted/deferred/conflicting receipts and an observation ledger. Reliable identity can update while a conflicting HP maximum remains unresolved. Ambiguous identity leaves unassigned HP as raw evidence rather than attaching it to an assumed occupant.
- `exact` in `recognition/entities.py` means a unique eligible normalized dictionary match, not independent visual verification. `text_icon_agreement` in `recognition/identity.py` means text and a resolved icon support the same identity. Fusion checks the raw name against the pinned knowledge dictionary, canonical species, resolution status/confidence, and independent icon conflicts; method strings alone do not suffice.
- A unique raw exact reading with confidence >= .95 can establish species immediately. A weaker exact reading >= .75 requires a prior consistent reading with confidence >= .75, or an explicit, unambiguous, >= .95-confidence same-side send-out announcement in the preceding 30 seconds. The latter may remain deferred as a full transition while independently supporting its named species. Support is recorded with source evidence. These are conservative software admission thresholds, not calibrated probabilities or verified game mechanics.
- The corroboration rule admits Rillaboom at 266.061667s from its .86 exact HUD reading plus the earlier explicit announcement at 260.06s. It does not backfill a slot at 260s. A future or stale announcement cannot corroborate an earlier/late reading; correction/retraction re-evaluates the support deterministically.
- Form evidence is admitted only when an independently resolved icon agrees on the canonical form or the raw exact name uniquely establishes that form. Bare species names with multiple forms do not assert a default form. Current observed form does not become a claim about native/original form.
- Same-slot, same-species occupancy observations preserve the current encounter instance until contrary evidence intervenes. A different occupant produces an observed occupancy episode, marking the prior location unknown rather than fabricating withdrawal, faint, damage, entry timing, or entry-trigger mechanics. An interval records the last applicable observation and the new observation time; its exact action time and cause stay unknown.
- Across absences, species equality is insufficient to merge individuals. Cached `team_instance_id` is not used as proof: recognition derives it by unique species matching against its roster. An explicit evidenced continuity relation can reconnect an existing instance and its revealed moves. Without such a relation, separate encounter episodes and their prior revelations are retained. Duplicate species on different sides/slots remain distinct.
- Parser grammar is unchanged. Opt-in messages are re-evaluated against the state at that replay prefix, including active species and evidenced nickname relations. Future rosters are never injected. Nickname support requires repeated high-confidence raw name plus independently resolved icon evidence; aliases are used only while that instance is active and the mapping is unambiguous. Disputed HUD slots cannot silently bind subsequent actors.
- Move execution now has a restricted `applied_partial` receipt when the actor binds uniquely to an observed active instance and the canonical move is reliable. Only actor/move fields are admitted. Target, damage, PP, exact slots, and move effects are not asserted. A selected move is not an executed/revealed move. Unknown actors or corrupt move identities remain unresolved.
- [tracking/hidden_state.py](../tracking/hidden_state.py) consumes that explicit partial receipt without promoting the whole OCR candidate to confirmed. It records move membership/provenance for the bound instance, with slots unknown. Existing confirmed-event and provisional-mechanics behavior is unchanged. Copied/temporary/transformed move origins retain the existing conditional-membership handling.
- Partial revelations skip mechanical handlers and knowledge-grounded effects. Consequently a later independent result cannot duplicate an effect from that announcement: the announcement applied none. Existing explicit causal receipts/deduplication for fully supported effects remain in place and are covered by the quantitative mechanics regression suite.

Large generated artifacts remain ignored by Git.

Run one explicit recording:

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/replay_m233a.py --battle-id sample_battle_4
```

The script requires completed cached survey/OCR stages, checks battle ownership, matching cached video fingerprints, and video size/mtime when the source recording is present. It hashes source observations, cached snapshots, knowledge/rule evidence, and relevant code. `.cache/battles/<battle_id>/m233a/pipeline-manifest.json` records stage/error status, input identity, and output checksums. Compatible completed battles are reused. Failures do not alter prior recognition stages or another battle’s outputs. Checkpoints are per battle, not per message; an interrupted incomplete fusion replay restarts that inexpensive replay for that battle.

Focused verification:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_evidence_fusion.py tests/test_m233a_cached.py
```

Cached tests have explicit `local_artifacts` prerequisites and skip when those generated outputs are unavailable in a clean clone. Portable fusion tests need no video/model/cache.

## Limitations and reserved B-stage work

- Sampling and OCR schedules were not changed. The observed glyph-gate misses, high-confidence corrupted move names, unsupported item-effect grammar, short-message sampling gaps, and incomplete Battle Log alignment from M2.3.2-D remain unresolved.
- Retrospective log events are not injected at log-viewing times into the live HUD/message timeline. Their existing separate reports remain available; action-time reconciliation needs explicit evidence rather than assigning historic actions to the moment a log was opened.
- Reappearing Pokémon are not automatically merged across observation gaps by species. Historical revelations stay on their encounter instances unless a reliable explicit continuity relation is provided. Current identity facts are last observed facts; an unresolved later HUD is logged as unresolved/conflicting and cannot supply a fresh actor binding.
- Native/original forms, hidden items/abilities, EVs/IVs/natures, exact move slots, unseen damage trajectories, switch causes, and unknown target effects remain unknown unless independently established. No new mechanical rules or probabilistic priors were introduced.
- Future M2.3.3-B should expose a single documented sampling setting targeting **2 FPS (0.5 seconds/frame)**, include it in cache fingerprints/manifests, and permit 3 FPS without restructuring. Frame acquisition frequency must remain separate from OCR invocation frequency. Reuse valid existing frames/recognition and measure runtime/OCR cost on a scoped recording before another five-video run. Faster UI/multi-hit sampling is future work. This policy is recorded in `AGENTS.md`; no B-stage setting or sampling implementation was added here.
