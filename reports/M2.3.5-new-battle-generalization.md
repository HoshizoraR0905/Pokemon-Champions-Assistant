## Implemented behavior and remaining gaps

- **Nickname/icon matching:** `recognition/icons.py` adds opt-in joint BGR
  correlation. Per-channel centering can discard palette differences; the new
  score centers all channels jointly and retains masked sprite support. Synthetic
  tests distinguish identical structure with different colors. Existing
  similarity, margin and nickname admission thresholds were not lowered. The
  opponent nicknames remain unresolved in this sample; no successful nickname
  generalization is claimed. The v2 matcher is not promoted as the default.
- **Move-line recovery:** `recognition/message_recovery.py` derives padding from
  line height and layout, not a move name or timestamp. Two supported cached
  configurations can recover a canonical suffix above the unchanged 0.90 move
  threshold. An execution prefix is required; guarding text and displayed moves
  do not become executed-move revelations.
- **Party-information panel:** `recognition/party_panel.py` requires a unique
  highlighted own-party row, literal captions and species/HP support. The visual
  adapter binds species, HP, ability and item to an observed episode without
  filling an active slot, inferring moves used, or applying item/ability effects.
  Field receipts and source provenance are retained. Positive held-out transfer
  for this layout remains untested.
- **Repeat suppression:** existing masked scheduling suppression is retained.
  `recognition/repeat_evidence.py` records each frame's source hash, time, mask
  hash, anchor and similarity. Fifteen suppressed observations remain explicit.
  Mask similarity asserts scheduling equivalence only; it does not copy OCR
  semantics or prove uninterrupted battle-state continuity.
- **HUD re-observation:** `recognition/hud_reuse.py` requires exact name/icon/HP
  crop bytes and keeps a new per-frame source receipt. No additional HUD snapshots
  qualified in this scoped run; all seven snapshots were reused from the baseline.
- **Identity and freshness:** opt-in `reliability_v2` separates current occupancy
  episodes across coverage gaps and preserves prior revelations historically.
  Recovery also closes the stale slotless-arrival path: a historical unplaced
  send-out cannot become a current individual solely from matching species after
  a gap. Correction/retraction regressions confirm continuity can be restored
  when evidence is corrected. Unknown identity remains unknown.
- **Projection compatibility:** existing M2.3.4 visual adapters remain usable.
  Added fainted/type/instance-continuity and action-attribution evidence fields
  are additive. The evidence projection remains disconnected from M3 calculations.

No runtime timestamps, Battle 5 species tables, reviewed answers, held-out labels,
learnset assumptions or probability priors were added to recognition/state logic.
Layout mappings and admission thresholds remain engineering assumptions, not
verified Champions mechanics.
