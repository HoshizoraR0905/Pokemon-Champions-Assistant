## 最早因果路径

- **first_refused_reentry** (`tracking/state_engine.py::_resolve(sending=True)`)：A partial-party species match rejected biological identity reuse and also prevented admitting a new active-entry episode. At 467.012s HUD created a new Rillaboom occupancy; historical aliases then competed with that current episode.
- **cross_side_withdrawal_alias** (`battle_events/parser.py::parse before substitutions`)：Comma recall grammar did not select self aliases before substitution. Trainer-prefixed withdrawal also selected the trainer side after alias lookup. 500.002s and 506.518s withdrawals lost their actor; subsequent entries had no reliable vacant slot.
- **inactive_alias_competition** (`tracking/evidence_fusion.py::prepare_message / identity_ledger aliases`)：Every historical alias competed with the current actor, including withdrawn instances; species identity and instance claims were collapsed. 527.022s faint lost Rillaboom identity despite one current opponent active episode.
- **unplaced_entry_poisoning** (`tracking/reliability.py::unplaced_entry / context_supported`)：An unresolved earlier departure left both slots apparently occupied. A later named entry became unplaced and gated the whole side. Moves, stat changes, fainting and further replacements became deferred.
- **unconfirmed_same_label_hud** (`tracking/evidence_fusion.py::episode_hud`)：A later unsuccessful identity acquisition with the same exact label blocked an independently established active episode. A failed HUD is not admitted by the repair; prior entry/occupancy evidence remains usable only without contradiction or a coverage gap.

## 队伍个体与入场 episode

四槽位与 PokémonState 仍是当前可观察状态的权威来源；`active_entry_episodes` 仅保存入场、放置、离场与证据历史。
队伍不完整时，同物种返回创建新的入场身份记录，并把旧非活动实例列为未证实的连续性候选；不会自动继承旧招式、状态或 HP，也不宣称它们必定是不同个体。旧记录及其历史保持可查。
有明确个体关联或独立验证的完整队伍且物种唯一时，可复用持久个体，同时创建新的入场 episode。旧 HP 作为历史证据保存，新 episode 的当前 HP 等待新观察。
