# Focused repair — opponent initial active identity binding

Completed as a saved-evidence diagnostic. Runtime changes are limited to `tracking/evidence_fusion.py` and `tracking/identity_ledger.py`; no recognition models, parser vocabulary, performance policy or historical predictions were changed. The repair was designed and checked using the saved 123–135s evidence. The subsequent whole-recording saved-event replay measures downstream impact only.

## Why a successfully read message could still be deferred

OCR answers what text was visible. State admission also needs evidence for the actor’s side, active instance and, when relevant, slot, target and effect. `deferred` preserves the text/provenance while withholding an unsupported mutation. Some HUD readings instead have a `recorded_observation` receipt whose nested identity result is deferred; these are counted separately below. The earlier viewer made this distinction easy to miss.

## Exact failure paths

1. `recognition/event_report.py:video_events` splits the named double-entry announcement into two `send_out` candidates. `EventParser.parse` resolves a trainer prefix only through `BattleContext.participants` (`battle_events/parser.py:78`). The occurrence parser used an empty context; replay’s `prepare_message` also constructed a fresh context without trainer observations. The double-entry children deliberately disable reparsing to preserve member identity, so their original `side_unknown` remained. Their `entry_slots` mapping therefore supplied no opponent slot.

2. `recognition/fast_profile.py:recognize_cards` resolves text and icons, then intentionally treats opponent strings as literal display labels: it calls `combine_identity` again with the text resolution cleared. It preserves the preceding resolution under `independent_text`, but the outer method becomes `icon_only`. At 134.007s the stored chain is `icon_only → text_icon_agreement → exact`; the exact leaf resolves each actual raw label to the same species as the separately computed icon.

3. `tracking/evidence_fusion.py:identity_evidence` previously accepted either a qualifying exact resolution, text/icon agreement, or a tightly controlled icon-only nickname. The nickname route explicitly required the raw label to be absent from the exact dictionary. These raw labels were present in it, and the outer method was neither `exact` nor `text_icon_agreement`. Both identities therefore fell through to `identity_not_independently_supported`. Retained readings then referenced originals that had never been admitted.

## Small corrections and safeguards

- `literal_label_icon_support` validates the saved independent exact-text leaf, the raw label’s unique species lookup, high OCR confidence, and the independently resolved primary icon and top candidate. Species/form conflicts, missing text, fused-only or fuzzy text, conditional evidence and conditioned icon methods do not qualify. Outer `icon_only` stays unchanged. Admission carries the separate policy `literal_label_independent_icon_agreement` and both source records. No `text_icon_agreement` label is manufactured. Existing form policy remains in force.

- `trainer_from_opening_hud` uses the existing `SideState.trainer` field. It associates a prior named double-entry’s literal label pair with exactly one of the two observed sides, requiring all four accepted HUD readings from the same frame, distinct labels, high-confidence entry text, and no intervening move/withdrawal/faint/transformation. Ambiguous or incomplete pairs do not create a claim. `prepare_message` supplies this evidenced, timestamped claim to the existing participant parser. No species, trainer names or video times are hardcoded in these runtime functions.

**No backdating:** the two original 123.020s send-out children remain unknown/deferred. Under the allowed evidence, trainer-side proof first exists at 134.007s. The correction establishes occupancy from the HUD at that point and resolves subsequent trainer-prefixed messages prospectively. It does not retroactively claim knowledge at 123.020s.

## Incorrect or unsupported admissions

No incorrect initial identity was found against the saved 123–135s evidence: all four slots have matching names, IDs, positions and HP. All 136 newly admitted full HUD readings carry the explicit independent-proof policy; 156 additional retained readings inherit a previously accepted episode under the existing retained-identity checks. No new receipt was rejected, and no previously accepted HUD identity was lost.

Negative regressions withhold absent/fuzzy/fused-only/conditional text, wrong species, wrong top candidates, conditional icon methods, low confidence and form conflicts. Trainer tests withhold missing/stale HUDs, the same pair on both sides, incomplete pairs, weak entry text, generic trainer labels and post-action associations; retraction removes the learned claim and separate battles do not share it.

This establishes policy support, not whole-match recognition accuracy. Later admitted observations have not all received independent human adjudication; zero rejected receipts does not prove zero recognition errors. No nickname-to-species or persistent re-entry equivalence was invented.
