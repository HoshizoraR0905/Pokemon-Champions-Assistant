## New interfaces

- `knowledge.stat_feasibility.champions_attainable_stats(...)` generates exact
  selected-stat domains with min/max, hull, disjoint intervals and gaps. It accepts
  partial known points, finite point domains, known/alternative factors, complete
  alignment alternatives, and exact/range/set constraints across all six stats.
  Omitted allocation/alignment information enumerates all documented alternatives.
- `damage.champions_feasibility.infer_champions_attack_stat(...)` accepts the
  existing numerical inverse request plus a `champions` constraint object. It
  preserves the entire original result in `damage_result` and reports
  `damage_feasible`, `champions_attainable`, `stat_intersection`, `final_feasible`,
  exclusions due only to stat legality, further exclusions due to joint constraints,
  compatible configurations, witnesses, sources and filtered scenario assignments.
- `python -m damage.champions_cli [request.json]` provides JSON composition, also
  accepting stdin. Exit 0 means completed exact/conditional/empty feasibility;
  exit 2 means unresolved, unsupported or invalid information.

M3.1.1's existing Python API, JSON command, schemas, fixtures and numerical outputs
remain unchanged. Composition calls its existing solver once; no damage evaluation
is duplicated to perform stat filtering. Known non-target actual attacker stats
and maximum HP in observations also constrain the single build. Null unused
stats stay unknown. Template placeholders that are not established actual values
must be null. The inferred-stat entry remains M3.1.1's designated placeholder.

Form identity must match every damage observation, with one established level
context. Stages may vary by observation. Scenario variables changing actual
attacker stats or maximum HP require separate explicit composition solves and
produce diagnostics here; they are not silently decoupled from training legality.

## Exact joint representation

For each permitted complete alignment, the existing utility generates at most
33 `(points, actual_stat, adjustment)` rows per stat. Actual-stat constraints filter
these domains. There is a joint allocation exactly when every domain is nonempty
and the sum of its six minimum point costs is at most 66. A particular row is
extendable exactly when its cost plus the other five minima is at most 66.

The complete feasible configuration set is returned as
`alignment-point-domains-budget-v1`: a union of alignment branches, choosing one
row for each stat with the explicit shared `sum(points) <= 66` restriction.
Independent combinations violating that inequality are not represented as legal.
This is an exact bounded constraint representation, not sampled/truncated builds.
Its bound is 21 × 6 × 33 input rows, avoiding an enormous six-stat Cartesian search.
One reproducible full witness per feasible value is also available; witnesses are
existence proofs, not preferred builds or probability estimates.

## Remaining limits

These results establish exact feasibility under the pinned documented stat model,
not official certification of every Champions mechanic. No unsupported rule is
silently generalized. The full training configuration is assumed unchanged across
combined observations. Arbitrary correlations beyond the returned per-alignment
point domains and budget require a richer explicit constraint model.

Stat legality does not validate damage attribution, identity continuity, move
learnability, equipped moves or regulation legality. The provisional forward
profile remains provisional. Unknown mechanics or missing identities yield
diagnostics rather than guessed legal builds.

No network is needed at runtime. No OCR/video processing, M2.3.x changes,
speed-order inference, Bayesian inference, general crawler or subsequent milestone
was implemented. No commit or push was performed. See
[the API and representation contract](../docs/champions-stat-feasibility.md).
