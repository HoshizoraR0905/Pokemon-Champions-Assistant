Date: 2026-10-21. Implementation and scoped validation complete; ready for review.
This is acceptance of the synthetic/reference subset, not recorded-battle or

## Audit and extension design

The clean starting tree was inspected progressively: M3.2 report, `core.py`,
`solver.py`, `scenarios.py`, original fixtures/tests, shared stat feasibility,
M3.4.1 `battle_evidence/actions.py`, `action_inference.py`, `order_mechanics.py`
and `speed_order/execution.py`. No recorded battle processing is needed.

M3.2 already supplies exact integer arithmetic, legal SP/alignment domains,
weather/terrain abilities, stages, paralysis, Tailwind, Trick Room, tie-inclusive
constraints, correlated finite variables, contradictions and bounded enumeration.
These implementations and v1 fixture expectations will be retained. Its single
unchanged-form/stat-epoch contract cannot represent transformations or dynamic
queues. Its priority abilities, conditional moves and action overrides are gated.

M3.4.1 already classifies priority hypotheses and has an opt-in probabilistic
execution adapter (including Quick Claw). Neither is a deterministic feasible-set
engine; neither will be changed or used for probabilities here. Its stable
instance IDs, action occurrence IDs, source provenance and local decision IDs
inform the additive v2 contract. No video-to-solver adapter is in scope.

The minimal extension is a versioned deterministic pending-action evaluator that
calls the existing effective-Speed calculator, plus a v2 dispatch in `infer_speed`.
Explicit finite hidden configurations remain shared across observations; local
state alternatives remain separate. Forms are current-state attributes of one
stable individual. Shared training constraints use `champions_attainable_stats`;
transformed stats use `ChampionsStats`, never a second stat formula.

## Mechanics evidence audit

Dynamic reevaluation of priority and Speed after each completed action is a user
requirement. The pinned Showdown Champions scripts declare generation 9;
`sim/battle.ts` updates all queued action speeds/priorities and sorts after actions
when generation >=8. This agrees for ordinary queued moves. Fractional priority
(including Quick Claw activation) is assigned in `battle-queue.ts` when resolving
the action, and is preserved during later sorting: activation must persist for
that action rather than being rerolled at each decision.

Quick Claw is a within-bracket order override, not a Speed multiplier or integer
move-priority change. The current 52Poké article explicitly includes Champions
item text describing its same-priority effect. Its probability text is outside
this deterministic implementation. An explicit activation message is a user
observation and also matches the pinned simulator activation event; independent
in-game message recognition has not been measured.

The maintained Champions catalog has Protect +4, Fake Out +3, Trick Room -7 and
Grassy Glide base 0. Pinned Showdown implements Prankster status +1, Gale Wings
Flying +1 at full HP, Triage heal-flag +3, and grounded Grassy Glide +1 on grassy
terrain. These are executable-reference rules, not independently certified
Champions timing/rounding. Custap Berry and Lagging Tail are marked Past in the
pinned Champions item overrides; Full Incense is Past in the inherited catalog.
They must not be silently offered as current legal items.

Official gameplay documentation checked today does not certify detailed queue
timing or rounding. 52Poké Champions learnsets were checked for Charizard,
Venusaur and Whimsicott; fetching Whimsicott's detailed table was incomplete, so
its Tailwind example remains explicitly synthetic/catalog based. Venusaur's
Champions table lists Grassy Glide and Giga Drain; Charizard's lists Flamethrower.
Learnability supplies no equipped-slot or probability evidence.

Sources and reproducible receipts will be recorded in
`speed_order/data/sources-v2.json`. The original numerical profile and >999 cap
discrepancy gate remain unchanged. Strict evidence-only numerical evaluation
remains unsupported.

## Implementation and compatibility

New modules are `speed_order/contracts.py` (typed evidence), `mechanics.py`
(priority/override separation), `forms.py` (shared training and form continuity),
`dynamic.py` (pending-action traces), and `conditional.py` (configuration-correlated
feasible sets). The existing `infer_speed` has an additive `schema_version=2`
dispatch; its v1 calculation loop is unchanged. `__init__.py` exports
`simulate_action_order` and `configuration_domain`; the JSON CLI adds `simulate`.
Those three files are the only previously tracked source files changed.

The detailed [v2 API contract](../docs/speed-order-v2.md), request/response schemas,
canonical-ID JSON examples under `examples/speed_order/v2/`, and versioned source
receipt provide standalone use without a recognition/state replay dependency.
Sources are only read locally at runtime. No competing identity catalog, stat
formula, metagame database or posterior engine was introduced.

Pending-action traces preserve every decision's before/after snapshots, pending
set, priority/override/Speed diagnostics, tied alternatives, activation assignment,
state-change provenance and exact application stage. Quick Claw assignments are
fixed per action. Unsupported branches and resource-limit exhaustion withhold
the complete result, rather than dropping hypotheses. Explicit cancellations
remove pending actions; interrupted/forced/skipped actions remain gated.

Inference intersects entire observations within each shared configuration/history
before unioning supports. Items, abilities, transformations and Unburden activation
history cannot be reselected independently to fit different turns. Local temporal
alternatives remain explicit. Known contrary priority evidence excludes a branch;
ambiguous priority explanations remain alongside ordinary-Speed explanations.
The output retains exact integer sets/gaps, compatible training/configuration
histories, initial and final ability/item hypotheses, subject tie compatibility,
per-form unmodified Speed, applied evidence and exclusions. Contradiction lists
are explicitly nonminimal. Configuration/form/effective Speeds remain separate.

## Remaining mechanics gaps and consequences

1. Dynamic ordinary queue timing is a user requirement corroborated by the pinned
   simulator, not independently verified Champions game behavior. Its generation-9
   queued-move refresh agrees with the requirement; fractional activation is fixed
   at queue resolution rather than recalculated after every action. Switches,
   replacements, residuals and other special queue entries are outside this module.
2. Numerical rounding/ties and the >999 cap discrepancy retain the original
   provisional profile and gates. The strict profile provides no numerical answer.
3. Only the documented closed move/ability subset is handled. Quick Draw,
   Mycelium Might, combined Stall/Quick Claw, forced ordering, preparation/multi-action
   moves, mid-queue Quick Claw acquisition, complex item/ability suppression locks
   and unhandled Mega ability side effects remain unresolved. They do not become
   neutral alternatives, and can block a complete feasible-set claim.
4. Roster/regulation legality and complete learnsets are not newly imported or
   certified. Caller-supplied candidate universes and pending-set coverage remain
   explicit prerequisites. Missing tables are not evidence of move impossibility;
   learnability does not establish slots or usage probabilities.
5. Large tie/activation/history products can exceed bounds. The API reports an
   unresolved full result; callers can supply tighter evidence or larger explicit
   supported limits. There is no posterior approximation or silent branch pruning.
6. No M3.4.1 video-to-solver adapter, recorded-video acceptance, Bayesian changes,
   M3.4 visual reconstruction changes, M3.4.2 damage inference or later milestone
   work was performed. Implementation and generated validation are complete for
   review; real-battle semantic acceptance remains separate.
