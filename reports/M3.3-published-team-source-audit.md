## Extraction and canonicalization boundaries

PokeDaily labels allocations **SP** in the visible sheet while its copyable export uses `EVs:` syntax. The adapter must corroborate the point line with that sheet's SP field before classifying it as source-asserted Champions SP. Generic EVs syntax alone remains `unknown` unless independently identified as `legacy_ev`. Unspecified point components are not defaulted from another record. A valid known allocation totaling the pinned 66-point budget deterministically leaves zero for unspecified components; retain that deduction as provenance. Otherwise missing components remain null.

Some Mega sheets describe both pre- and post-Mega abilities; preserve the exact exported form and ability and flag cross-surface disagreement/phase ambiguity. Do not silently substitute a base species or alternate ability to force a match. Canonical IDs come exclusively from the existing KnowledgeBase. Unknown forms, moves/items/abilities and unsupported gameplay fields prevent exact-build acceptance; known six-form compositions may remain usable.

New publications retain source URL, stable on-page locator, original page hash, original export/card data, author/rental/date evidence, population, declared format/regulation and extraction version. Content revisions are separate source records; repeat downloads of identical content are not new publications. No independent creator/game verification or current rental-code validity is claimed.

## Selected architecture

Extend `metagame` with an isolated `published` subpackage and its own versioned SQLite database. Reuse hashing, atomic manifests, the HTTP adapter, exact shared identities and pinned point validation. Preserve all immutable publication revisions and link them to independently versioned composition and exact-build fingerprints. Never run the Ranked cache's retention pruner over this database. A bounded pilot limits collection volume without deleting verified publications.

The primary observation is one distinct design. `unique_build` counts complete exact builds once; `unique_composition` counts six-form multisets once. Keep distinct children under shared compositions and publication multiplicity as unweighted metadata. Require explicit population/regulation/format selection in queries; never pool tournaments/community/Ranked or fingerprint versions silently. Unknowns cannot establish exact equality. Partial records retain their evidence and candidate duplicates without merging. No approximate SP-distance family grouping, online inference, M3.4, or Ranked encounter-probability interpretation is authorized.
