# M3.4 battle evidence infrastructure — validation

Implemented a separate read-only reconstruction package with turn grouping,
temporary-effect duration support, verified own-build access, immutable prefix
snapshots, conservative HP attribution, and the M3.4.1 adapter. The
[pre-implementation gap audit](../docs/M3.4-evidence-gap-audit.md) and
[interface/recovery guide](../docs/battle-evidence-v1.md) describe the contracts.
M2.4 and existing calculators/inference/database modules were not rewritten.

**Real-data result:** three partial historical turn contexts were recovered; no
complete live turn boundary pair was established. There are 79 raw integer-display
HP transitions, zero verified attack damage measurements, zero newly eligible
M3.4.1 updates and zero actual posterior information gain. This is an honest
infrastructure/evidence result, not recognition accuracy or semantic acceptance of
all saved events. Synthetic successes are reported separately.

## Source audit and own-team information

The original Sample Battles 5–9 videos are present. This task reused existing
sampled source frames, OCR/events, replay receipts and historical log artifacts;
no new video decoding, frame extraction or Sample Battle OCR was performed. Representative move
frames from all five recordings were visually inspected. Additional cached log
frames were reviewed at B5 230.005 s, B6 474.0133333333333 s and B8 327.005 s.
Small [agent-reviewed excerpts](../tests/fixtures/battle_evidence/real-log-excerpts.json)
retain source hashes, displayed headings and incomplete rows. They are not
independent human ground truth or an automatic recognition benchmark.

The selected live input runs are the same as the M3.4.1 audit. Additional inputs
include B6 `m238-live-2/reference/battle-log.json` (six entries under heading 4;
its recovery-run copy repeats the same entries) and source recognition metadata.
B8 `summary-log.json` is a processing summary, not additional game log entries.
Historical log inspection time remains distinct from unknown original action time.

`data/teams/my_team.json` contains six incomplete members, with no manually verified
fields. `data/teams/sample_battle_3_opening.json` contains reviewed fields for a
**different battle**, but no verified binding to Battles 5–9. `.cache/team-profiles`
is absent. Preview species/HP and similar rosters do not establish a configured
build. All five experiments therefore have **zero bound verified own builds and
zero action snapshots with sufficient own Speed evidence**. Unknown SP, alignment,
items, abilities, levels/settings and equipped move slots remain unknown.
`KnownBuilds` can consume existing reviewed TeamConfiguration/TeamProfiles with
explicit instance bindings; it reuses ChampionsStats for all six unmodified stats
and M3.2 for supported effective Speed. Full combat modifiers are not replaced by
stage-adjusted stats when their supporting action context is missing.

The duration policy uses applicable 52Poké Champions-specific descriptions for
[Tailwind](https://wiki.52poke.com/index.php?title=顺风&oldid=2606453) and
[Trick Room](https://wiki.52poke.com/index.php?title=戏法空间&oldid=2554174), together
with their documented inclusive activation turn. These support 4/5-turn budgets;
bookkeeping occurs after confirmed end-of-turn boundaries. Relative ordering of
individual residual effects is not inferred. This is documented community evidence,
not official in-game certification. The checked Champions
[Damp Rock](https://wiki.52poke.com/index.php?title=潮湿岩石&oldid=2622423) description
supports an extended 8-turn rain case, but does not establish a recorded setter's
item or activation mode. Other durations/extension supports require explicit applicable
source evidence. Generic Generation 9 rules are not silently substituted.

The versioned `battle_evidence/duration-rules-v1.json` retains URLs, revisions,
retrieval date 2026-10-20, rendered hashes, 52Poké attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0
licensing. Rendered snapshots stay in `.cache/m34e/sources/`; revision IDs alone do
not freeze transcluded templates. Unknown durations remain unknown even though
controlled tests can exercise declared 5/8-turn duration supports.

## Controlled traces: full turns, countdowns and clean damage

Separately labeled synthetic tests establish consecutive turn starts/ends and exact
membership/order. Opposing Tailwind activated within turn 1 follows:

| Instant | Opposing Tailwind remaining support | Active |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turn 1, after verified activation / during action | {4} | true |
| After turn 1 end / turn 2 start | {3} | true |
| After turn 2 end / turn 3 start | {2} | true |
| After turn 3 end / turn 4 start | {1} | true |
| After turn 4 end | {0} | false |
| Verified new activation in turn 5 | {4} | true |

Self-side activation is tracked independently. Missing end boundaries can be accounted
for once at a confirmed consecutive next-turn start; gaps widen support. Repeated
boundaries do not double-decrement. Additional tests exercise Trick Room, declared
variable weather/terrain/screen durations, extensions, expiration, replacement and
unknown activation. These validate bookkeeping under declared rules, not recognition.

A separate synthetic own HP 153→100 transition, with one verified damaging move,
attacker/recipient/target membership, resolution snapshot, a source position between
readings, complete causal coverage and no multi-hit/redirection/substitute, emits
exact loss 53 as a damage-input-eligible observation. Residual/healing/recoil/self-loss
remain distinct and never become attack damage. Status moves and censored fainting
losses cannot pass that contract. No inverse/offensive-stat posterior is calculated.
The Speed adapter likewise yields a genuine controlled update only with supported
same-decision state, verified own build and explicit provisional mechanics profile;
unknown priorities/modifiers and correlated/repeated evidence are excluded by the
existing M3.4.1 engine.

## Replay compatibility and runtime

No source replay/cache is overwritten. One new prefix-projection stage was generated
per battle where missing, using the current maintained StateEngine. Runtime and
end-state comparisons:

| Battle | New replay/projection stage (s) | Full final hash matches saved | Compact final projection matches saved | Latest downstream reconstruction (s) |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| sample_battle_5 | 61.630 | False | True | 2.503 |
| sample_battle_6 | 286.548 | False | True | 6.979 |
| sample_battle_7 | 172.351 | False | False | 3.684 |
| sample_battle_8 | 28.845 | True | True | 0.840 |
| sample_battle_9 | 27.248 | True | True | 1.868 |

Full-state hashes differ in B5–7. The compact final battle-fact projections match
for B5/B6; this does not prove full-state equality or every earlier prefix. B7 has
20 differing projected paths, including opposing occupancy/instance IDs and own
status facts. Its prefix snapshots are explicitly provisional current-code replay
views, with saved/current hash and path warnings attached; they must not be treated
as matching historical states. These differences were exposed without changing
StateEngine, repairing old caches or substituting identities. B8/B9 match both final
hash and compact projection. Final agreement alone is not recognition accuracy.

Completed projection stages are reused independently of downstream duration,
annotation and attribution changes. The initial B5 downstream failure on an
unresolved terrain identity preserved its successful replay stage; the resumed
reconstruction kept that identity unknown and reused the completed projection.
Progress checkpoints, errors, inputs, code/dependency hashes and output hashes are
recorded in manifests. Detailed artifacts remain local. `.cache/m34e/battle-index.json`
records the final five outputs; identical reruns check their hashes and reuse them.

## Validation, preservation and remaining requirements

**49 new deterministic infrastructure cases** cover consecutive/partial/ambiguous
turns; command/battle phases and forced replacement; side-specific Tailwind,
activation/decrement/expiration/reapplication and gaps; Trick Room; declared variable
weather/terrain/screen durations and extensions; snapshots; reviewed own stats and
missing/conflicting builds; individual continuity; exact single/spread/multi-hit
attack attribution; multiple causes; residual/healing/recoil/self-loss; unknown and
censored HP changes; state/move/target prerequisites; Speed integration from both
structured snapshot facts and explicit decision contexts; priorities and hidden
modifiers; intervening switches/item changes; duplicate/correlated/retracted/revised evidence; profile
mismatch; deterministic replay; historical time-domain separation; replay drift;
checkpoint reuse/invalidation and recovery after downstream failure.

Final portable regression command:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q -m 'not local_artifacts' tests/test_battle_evidence.py tests/test_speed_inference.py tests/test_champions_stats.py tests/test_champions_feasibility.py tests/test_speed_order.py tests/test_damage.py tests/test_inverse_damage.py tests/test_hidden_state.py tests/test_git_artifact_policy.py tests/test_metagame.py tests/test_published_teams.py
```

Result: **1,213 passed, 2 deselected**. Separate existing TeamConfiguration,
TeamProfiles and stateful-mechanics compatibility checks also passed. That separate
run included the optional OCR regression on **two curated configuration screenshots**
and emitted one existing optional ccache warning. No Sample Battle video OCR or
decoding was rerun. Synthetic tests do not certify automatic recognition reliability.

Preservation baseline: 588 pre-existing tracked/source/test/input/database files
were fingerprinted before implementation. **587 are unchanged**; the sole expected
change is `pyproject.toml`, registering this package/CLI/data file while preserving
the pre-existing M3.4.1 registration. Both M3.3 SQLite databases, original selected
recognition/replay/manifest files, existing inference/calculator modules and unrelated
working-tree edits are preserved. Additional historical inputs carry their own
source hashes. `git diff --check` passes. No artifacts were deleted; nothing was
staged, committed or pushed.

What still prevents real inference:

1. Reliable live turn boundary/continuity and order-decision evidence, rather than
   command-cycle timestamps or historical log inspection time.
2. Verified own build-to-individual bindings and contemporaneous modifier states;
   unreadable or missing configuration must remain unknown.
3. Applicable, supported Champions ordering mechanics; M3.4.1's strict profile
   still declines independently uncertified numerical ordering/tie calculations.
4. Complete damage-resolution/target and HP-cause coverage, including animation,
   multiple hits/causes, substitutes/redirection and residual/item/ability effects.
5. Review/version-compatible replay evidence where preserved and current observable
   state differ, especially B7. No uncertain individual identities are merged.

The APIs can consume stronger verified evidence now, and controlled scenarios
exercise successful countdowns, exact HP attribution and Speed updates. Actual
cached observations remain excluded when these prerequisites are missing. M3.4.2,
empirical priors, live integration and strategy work have not been started.
