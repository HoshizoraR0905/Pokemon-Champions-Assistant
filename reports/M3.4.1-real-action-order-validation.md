Why numeric updates remain zero:

1. Configured builds do not establish the current Mega form, ability suppression/transfer, consumed/changed item state, status, Speed stages or Unburden history. Source identity splits also remain separate rather than being silently merged. Some own actors still lack reliable individual or species bindings.
2. Saved StateEngine snapshots contain last-observed facts and explicit unknowns. No recording has a verified complete order-decision snapshot and contemporaneous modifier continuity. Counter readings cannot certify Tailwind side identity or an otherwise unknown condition.
3. Intervening effects, transformations, switches and Speed changes require source-backed queue-context review. The pinned simulator dynamically reorders; independently verified Champions timing is still missing.
4. Different or hypothesis-dependent priorities, special moves and opponent/opponent comparisons are preserved with their specific exclusion/defer reasons. Unknown item/ability domains are never replaced with neutral assignments.
5. Strict `champions-evidence-only-v1` separately refuses numerical inference. Selecting the conditional reference profile alone does not repair missing observation evidence.

Legal unmodified-Speed supports and non-empirical prior-only summaries are exported for opponent source instances with resolved current forms. They are explicitly not posterior updates. Unknown forms receive no fabricated base-form support. Posterior MAP/mean/intervals and item weights are produced by the existing engine only when evidence passes admission.
