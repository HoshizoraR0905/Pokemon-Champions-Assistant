# M3.4.1 Bayesian opponent Speed inference — validation

Implemented an offline configuration-level Bayesian inference module, evidence
ledger, identity-keyed belief bank, reproducible CLI, and cached Battles 5–9 audit.
No empirical M3.3 priors, damage inference, online integration or strategy logic
were added. No commits or pushes were made. See [interface and model policy](../docs/speed-inference-v1.md).

**Acceptance boundary:** controlled inference works under the explicitly provisional
Champions executable-reference profile. Actual recordings yield **zero eligible
comparisons and zero measured Speed information gain**. This is an evidence audit
and implementation validation, not recognition-accuracy validation or independent
certification of Champions ordering mechanics.

## Controlled Bayesian experiment

The portable example uses base Charizard, neutral numerical alignment and Speed
SP {0,1,2}, giving Speed {120,121,122}, each with prior weight ⅓. These restricted
SP/alignment values are declared synthetic conditions, not estimates from any
recording. Both actors have explicit native Blaze, no item, stage 0, no Tailwind
or status, no weather/terrain/Trick Room or ordering exception. Both ordinary
Flamethrower actions have priority 0 at a known ordering snapshot. Each observation
has distinct action IDs and a different declared turn. The own unmodified Speeds
121 and 122 are legal under the maintained stat rules.

| Stage | Observed condition | Likelihood over prior Speed {120,121,122} | Posterior | Mean | MAP | 95% interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Prior | Declared synthetic model | — | {120:⅓,121:⅓,122:⅓} | 121 | 120,121,122 | 120–122 |
| Turn 1 | Opponent before own Speed 121 | {0,½,1} | {121:⅓,122:⅔} | 121⅔ | 122 | 121–122 |
| Turn 2 | Opponent before own Speed 122 | {0,0,½}; dead candidates remain dead | {122:1} | 122 | 122 | 122–122 |

Turn 1 predictive likelihood is ½. Turn 2 conditional predictive likelihood is ⅓.
Final Speed-marginal information gain relative to this prior is ln(3) =
1.0986122886681098 nats. This is genuine information gain **within the synthetic
conditional reference model**, not a measured result from a Champions recording.
A subsequent own-Speed-120-first observation contradicts the singleton support:
all likelihoods are zero, the posterior becomes undefined, and last-consistent
weights remain explicitly diagnostic. Tests verify that no reset or smoothing occurs.

## Validation and reproducibility

The focused deterministic tests cover strict/reference separation; faster/slower/tie;
Trick Room; Tailwind; paralysis; stage +1; known Chlorophyll/Unburden activation;
unknown modifiers and unsupported branches; finite hidden Scarf alternatives;
priority differences; ambiguous move/actor evidence; same individual after a switch
gap; separate same-species individuals, battles and epochs; form changes; duplicate,
revised, correlated and out-of-order evidence; contradictions; nonuniform Speed
marginals; SP budget; normalization and numerical underflow; snapshot replay;
CLI output; no metagame/recognition/tracking imports or network in inference; and
cache checkpoint reuse/source invalidation. Controlled tests do not certify game
mechanics. Existing numerical regressions retain their original evidence scope.

Reproduce without new video work:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference controlled --output .cache/m341/controlled.json
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference infer examples/speed_inference/controlled.json --output .cache/m341/reproduced.json
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference audit --battle 5
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference audit --battle 6
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference audit --battle 7
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference audit --battle 8
.venv/bin/python -m speed_inference audit --battle 9
```

Each scoped audit writes `.cache/battles/<battle_id>/m341/<fingerprint>/manifest.json`
and `audit.json`. Matching complete outputs are reused after checking source,
implementation/dependency and output hashes. Failed stages preserve an error in
the manifest; resume the same command. Earlier fingerprints remain untouched.
The compact `.cache/m341/battle-audit-index.json` records this validation's paths.
Detailed generated snapshots remain local and are not added to Git.

Remaining limits: no independently verified Champions tie/ordering model; closed
M3.2 handler subset; no reliable recorded comparison yet; native ability/form
legality depends on the pinned community catalog; and identity fragmentation in
saved states is not resolved by this milestone. Enabling actual recorded updates
requires evidence-backed turn/action pairing, own build/Speed, contemporaneous
modifier and priority states, and applicable Champions mechanics verification.
M3.4.2 and later work have not been started.
