## Joint filtering and item conclusions

[battle_evidence/offensive.py](../battle_evidence/offensive.py) consumes the
existing Speed configurations, form-training support and admitted survivor IDs.
Damage restricts SP domains inside each persistent row. A dynamic-programming
count retains the shared ≤66 budget across all six stats; it never combines a
Speed configuration from one build with offense from another. Tests include
Speed SP 32 with HP SP 32, leaving at most two Attack SP, and two observations
whose independently feasible builds have no common joint survivor.

Every retained configuration needs at least one matching raw integer roll.
Multiple observations intersect the same IDs, alignment, item and training
domains. Unsupported branches or evaluation limits retain their configurations
and reasons; completeness/necessary-item claims are withheld. Cross-form damage
epochs require explicit transport and are deferred. Damage propagation through
cross-individual binary Speed witnesses is also explicitly gated, rather than
silently replacing the relation with independent marginal sets. The accepted
Raichu case does not involve such a binary Speed relation.

This case needs **no additional offensive item or ability boost**. Raichunite Y
and No Guard were already established by the confirmed transformation; they are
not newly inferred as offensive modifiers. The generic result distinguishes
possible modifiers, configurations without an extra boost, and modifier choices
required only when every supported survivor and relevant alternative is covered.
Necessary-item conclusions are conditional on an exhaustive supplied reference
domain, never descriptive published-build frequencies.
