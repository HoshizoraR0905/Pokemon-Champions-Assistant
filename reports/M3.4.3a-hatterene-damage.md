## Copying repair, storage and measured resource behavior

The old binding expanded 8,316 Speed rows into **347,193 concrete item rows**,
deep-copying their six-stat lists. The solver deep-copied this domain again and
recomputed each prefix. Full trace/provenance trees were also retained per forward
calculation, and packaging duplicated training and unresolved-ID arrays.

The implementation now:

- Keeps item alternatives on compact Speed rows and splits only when their SP feasibility or supported/unresolved state differs. Concrete IDs remain `speed_parent_id@item` even when compact groups split.
- Uses copy-on-write training mappings and immutable, JSON-compatible point lists. Stable Speed parent IDs, alignment and the joint SP budget remain attached.
- Filters chronological observations once, with independently chosen random/critical alternatives, and snapshots persistent survivors after each hit.
- Memoizes only required forward outputs. Keys include the complete request/field epoch plus every varied form, ability, item, stat, consumed defender item and alternative; changed observations cannot reuse sealed prefixes.
- Caches DP counts by immutable six-domain values, including budget changes.
- Stores shared training and repeated issue-ID sets once in replay schema `own-hp-offensive-v2`. `training_ref` and `configuration_ids_ref` resolve in `offensive_inference`; checkpoints retain inline solver records. Singular `offensive` UI fields remain available. No other production consumer of inline replay configurations was found.
- Streams large atomic JSON writes and seals per-hit checkpoints before replay publication. A compatible interrupted build resumes from its verified prefix; changed/corrupt present evidence raises an error.
