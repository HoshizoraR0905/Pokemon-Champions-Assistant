## Data and conclusions

Existing source scope: 1,083 published VGC M-C Doubles teams, 6,498 member records,
3,392 complete occurrences. Existing compatible-core filtering leaves 3,388
four-move association observations. Primary counts are Hatterene 13, Incineroar
197 and compatible Mega Raichu Y 72. Four conflicting Raichu source abilities
are excluded by the established adapter, with original IDs and reasons retained.
No collection, videos, OCR or new knowledge-source retrieval was needed.

The descriptive default screen retains K=1 for Hatterene and Incineroar, and
K=2 (46/26) for Raichu Y. All K candidates remain inspectable, including the
seven-observation high-Attack Incineroar neighborhood. These are factual
associations, not forced tactical labels. Learned moveset association does not
improve any numeric held-out prediction target over SP-only here. Incineroar
item error changes from SP-only 0.381 to learned 0.305, compared with training
mode 0.310: a small exploratory gain, not calibrated generalization evidence.

Read [the complete study](M3.5.1-build-similarity.md) for all ablations, cluster
sizes, full medoid identities/builds, rare-pair diagnostics, projections, grouped
holdout results and limitations. Hatterene has only 13 observations and constant
Attack/Speed SP; Raichu Y has constant Attack SP. Three resamples/folds do not
provide precise uncertainty estimates.
