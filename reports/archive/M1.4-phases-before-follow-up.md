## Efficiency, provenance, and reproduction

`video_frames(path, interval=1.0, limit=None)` accepts a frame-count limit, not a start time, and grabs frames sequentially. Its signature was checked before reuse. OpenCV timestamp seeks failed to retrieve the requested late frames. Two FFmpeg input seeks (`-ss` before `-i`, one frame each) produced the 520- and 720-second review fixtures; neither video was processed in full. A local `imageio-ffmpeg` helper was installed in `.venv` to access FFmpeg, without adding a runtime dependency. [Extraction records](M1.4-seek-review.json) include requested times, commands, frame hashes, review notes, and tool version. Requested timestamps are seek targets, not claimed exact frame timestamps.
