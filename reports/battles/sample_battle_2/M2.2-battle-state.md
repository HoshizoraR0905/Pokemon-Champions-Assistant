# M2.2 — Incremental Battle State Tracker

Validated on 2026-10-14 by resuming the interrupted working tree. The existing tracker, fixture, evidence images, and replay script were retained. The missing work was independent validation, regression tests, and this report; no state-transition handler required replacement for the supplied evidence.

## Implementation status

- `tracking/battle_state.py`: battle, side, Pokémon instance, HP, item, ability, move, stat-stage, status, effect, duration, and transformation models. Facts distinguish unknown, observed, derived, conflicting, and hypothesis categories. Unknown is separate from zero, false, or observed absence. Hidden configuration fields remain unknown.
- `tracking/state_engine.py`: incremental event ingestion with replay from the initial state; stable instance IDs; explicit ordering; provenance and binding records; atomic transitions; rejection/conflict records; corrections and retractions with revision history; serialization and deterministic replay.
- Existing handlers cover withdrawal/send-out, faint, HP endpoints, move executions, stat changes/reset/replacement, major statuses, ability activation/modification/suppression, item activation/revelation/consumption/removal/transfer, transformation/resource use, volatile conditions, weather, terrain, Trick Room, side conditions/hazards, phase observations, and explicit turn boundaries.
- Effectiveness and move-effect messages are retained as observations. Item/ability activation and move execution do not manufacture damage, stat changes, consumption, or secondary effects. Intermediate HP samples do not replace endpoints.
- `battle_events/schema.py` adds the tracker event types while preserving the existing recognition modules and event parser.
- `scripts/replay_m22.py` consumes only the saved M2.1 JSON and initial-state JSON. It emits final JSON, text, and 20 per-event snapshots, and checks duplicate delivery and serialized replay.
- `tests/test_battle_state.py` adds 21 offline tests including parametrized cases. No OCR or video decoding is involved in these tests.

No separate original M2.2 specification was found in the repository. Completion is assessed against the resume requirements and the existing implementation's scope; broader requirements outside those sources cannot be independently certified.

## Limitations and unresolved scope

- This validates reconstruction against the saved 20 candidates and manually annotated initial context, not the independent truth of every OCR-derived candidate. Candidate confidence and uncertainty remain visible in provenance.
- Deduplication is by logical event ID. Cross-source semantic deduplication is not implemented. A different payload with the same ID requires explicit correction.
- Incomplete/duplicate-species rosters and unresolved variants require explicit binding when identity is ambiguous. The tracker does not infer unseen team members or automatically equate an opponent's later same-species send-out with an earlier instance.
- Unknown durations are never filled with default game durations. Known durations tick only with explicit supported boundaries/timing; a skipped-turn boundary invalidates duration counts rather than fabricating intermediate ticks.
- Stat-reset and volatile-persistence mechanics are not inferred on withdrawal; callers must supply explicit reset/removal events. Bench HP and statuses are last observed facts. Zero HP and a faint event are separate evidence channels; an HP endpoint alone does not synthesize a faint event.
- Conflicting proposed transitions are rejected atomically and recorded in the conflict ledger. Per-field conflicting facts can be represented, but automatic conflict adjudication or hypothesis inference is not implemented.
- Supplemental handlers are covered with synthetic evidence; the real 20-event sample does not exercise all of them. This is a standalone tracker/replay layer, not a live OCR-to-state integration milestone.
- No M2.3 work, damage calculation, Bayesian inference, or strategic recommendations were added. No unresolved failure remains in the tested M2.2 scope; the unexecuted OCR/video tests and unavailable separate original specification remain explicit validation limits.
