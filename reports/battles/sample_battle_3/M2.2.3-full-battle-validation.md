## Evaluation conclusion and limits

**This recording does not support a fully automatic, unattended reconstruction with the current implementation.** Recognition obtains substantial usable Chinese text, and the state engine/viewer can represent the resulting multi-turn history. Scroll overlap gaps, nickname bindings, selected-party initialization, some compound messages, status-panel details, and Turn 8 video-only transitions require explicit annotation/reconciliation.

The corrected replay is an end-to-end evidence-supported validation of the existing architecture, not independent perfect ground truth. It preserves automatic partial results, documents human intervention, and exposes uncertainty. The seven decision-screen HP/identity checkpoints and deterministic replay demonstrate consistency at those checkpoints; they do not establish the accuracy of every intermediate field or a general success rate on other recordings.

## Deferred follow-up issues — not implemented in M2.2.3

### 1. Initialize complete known own-Pokémon configuration

Initialize our Pokémon from the opening team setup and existing team database, rather than rediscovering already known stats and moves during battle. Bind the configuration to stable instance IDs and the observed four-member selection; keep the six-member preview roster distinct from the selected party. Retain the configuration version and source evidence for known stats, moves, items, abilities, and other available configuration fields. Flag mismatches between setup evidence and database records instead of silently selecting one.

Keep that known configuration separate from changing battle facts such as current HP, PP, stat stages, suppression, and transformations. Continue observing those changes, and preserve unknown opponent configurations. This milestone's partial menu-based revelations and manual initialization remain in place; the complete configuration import is deferred.

### 2. Resolve quantitative effects through versioned move-mechanics knowledge

Use a versioned move-mechanics knowledge base to resolve quantitative effects, including Swords Dance's Attack stage increase. Each effect needs explicit rule-profile applicability, source/verification metadata, and activation conditions; unsupported Champions details must remain provisional rather than becoming unconditional defaults. M2.2.3 still leaves some textual stat-change magnitudes unresolved.

Associate a move-derived effect and a separately observed stat-change message with the same actor and action occurrence. Apply the stage increase once, retaining both sources as evidence; the later message should corroborate or refine the effect rather than apply a second delta. Conflicting observed and rule-derived values must stay explicit. This knowledge-base integration and effect reconciliation are deferred; neither was implemented during finalization.
