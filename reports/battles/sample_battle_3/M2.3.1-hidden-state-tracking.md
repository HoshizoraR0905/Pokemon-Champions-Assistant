# M2.3.1 — Evidence-based opponent hidden-state tracking

## Result and boundaries

A separate deterministic `HiddenEngine` now tracks factorized opponent configuration constraints by the existing Pokémon instance IDs. It consumes normalized events and accepted instance bindings, preserves evidence across switches, and supports incremental application, full replay, corrections, retractions, duplicate delivery and serialization. It does not change the observable StateEngine or calculate probabilities, damage, Speed order, EVs or recommendations.

The cached 196-event M2.2.4 recording is enriched in conservative and explicitly opted-in reference modes. All 197 observable prefix states are exactly equal to their original counterparts against the **matching M2.2.4 rules profile**, after removing the added hidden namespace and read-only Pokémon projection, including their provenance. Both replays retain **28/28 HP** and **28/28 identity** checkpoints. Existing M2.2.3/M2.2.4 artifacts are preserved. No video decoding, OCR, annotation extraction or model training was performed.

## Schema and evidence policy

`tracking/hidden_state.py` defines `Constraint`, `HiddenRules` and `HiddenEngine`. Each instance has factorized fields for species, original form, historical Mega capability, held-item identity, starting item, native/effective ability knowledge, move membership/observed slots, nature, training allocation, IVs, maximum HP and the five other individual stats. Temporal item/ability facts are keyed to their observation or transformation event. Exact slot numbers are retained only when supplied by a supported observation.

Domain statuses are `confirmed`, `constrained`, `possible`, `unknown`, `conflicting`; provisional results are stored separately with `provisional_inference`, so an unverified deduction cannot silently replace confirmed evidence. Hard and provisional contradictions remain visible, including proposed constraints and the last consistent domain. Configuration does not contain current HP, PP, stages, active/bench state, item consumption flags or ability suppression. `dynamic_reference` links those facts back to the existing observable tracker.

A constraint retains its ID, instance, variable, operation, source event IDs, evidence status, strength, optional rule/profile, provenance, prerequisites and elimination reason. Direct caller assertions without an event source are explicitly labeled as caller assertions. Supported operations are equality, exclusions, finite candidate sets, numeric intervals, membership, observed slots, conditional implications and disjunctions. Candidate eliminations and narrowed intervals retain the responsible constraint and reason. Contradictory intersections are never silently accepted as a solved empty domain.

Propagation handles independent factors and explicit relations without enumerating nature/training/IV/moveset products or using a SAT solver. A confirmed prerequisite can activate a conditional consequence; contradictory prerequisites retract that consequence. Disjunctions retain alternatives and record exclusions caused by conflicting confirmed fields. No applicable branch produces a visible conflict. Reference-derived observations cannot become hard constraints in the conservative profile.

Increasing event delivery applies incrementally. Out-of-order delivery, correction and retraction rebuild deterministically. Invalid event/correction payloads roll back. External knowledge observations retain their chronological insertion boundaries, allowing their provenance and ordering to survive replay. Queries use stored domains and constraints rather than rescanning raw battle history. Legacy observable envelopes restore an empty hidden layer instead of acquiring guessed defaults.

`HiddenEngine.decision_projection()` exposes confirmed fields, remaining/unknown domains, unresolved requirements, conflicts and supporting constraints. The existing `StateEngine.decision_state(hidden_engine=...)` accepts this optional projection; callers that omit it retain the legacy output. Its belief status explicitly says deterministic constraints, with no probability distribution.

Repeated move execution adds evidence without adding another moveset member or displaying another new-move revelation. Unmarked ordinary executions establish observed configuration membership. Explicit copied/called/external/temporary origins and generic transformation move changes instead create unresolved membership records. Selected or merely displayed moves are not executions; a displayed configuration can establish membership/slot only when its configuration status is explicitly confirmed.

The replay does not infer Unburden from White Herb or a Seed. It does not use absent activations as ability exclusions. Intimidate is confirmed as an **observed effective ability**, while original/native ability remains conditional unless its origin is established. Mega Salamence's post-transformation effective ability is not silently filled with Aerilate from a catalog. Native ability constraints, when genuinely established, survive replacements and switches.

White Herb identity is retained as past possession evidence. Its activation does not prove consumption or continued possession. `held_item` describes the observed identity in the first tracked possession epoch, not a claim that it was held at battle start. Starting-item knowledge is a separate field. Explicit item receipt/replacement/transfer begins another epoch, preventing legitimate exchanges from appearing as incompatible original-item equalities. Different identity observations within the same epoch produce a visible conflict. Current possession remains the observable tracker's fact.

All opposing maximum HP and absolute individual stats remain unknown: the recording supplies percentage HP endpoints, not reliable exact maxima. Stage changes do not constrain investment. Nature, training allocations and IVs are not estimated. No action-order inequality or damage inference was introduced.

## Mega side attribution and item requirements

This recording demonstrates **both** sides:

- Turn 1: own Froslass becomes Mega Froslass (`sample_battle_3:self:preview:6`).
- Turn 5: opposing Sally / Salamence becomes Mega Salamence (`sample_battle_3:opponent:encounter:4fa73de0b48a145a`).

The own transformation never creates an opponent item constraint. Both transformations establish their observed form and historical Mega capability, independently of future resource availability. The original form and current form remain separate.

`data/hidden_rules_v1.json` contains two narrowly scoped prerequisite rules. Under **`showdown-champions-reference-v1`**, an unambiguous Mega Salamence transformation provisionally requires Salamencite (`pokeapi:item:810`) immediately before that transformation. Mega Froslass similarly provisionally requires Froslassite (`pokeapi:item:2240`). Neither deduction asserts battle-start possession, current continued possession or consumption. The own opening Froslassite configuration remains separately available from M2.2.4.

Under the default **`champions-evidence-only-v1`**, these numerical/item-mechanic rules are unavailable; transformation requirements remain unresolved. An ambiguous target form, wrong species, or unsupported transformation never selects a specific stone. Multiple possible mechanisms are represented as a disjunction rather than a chosen explanation. Synthetic tests cover alternative mechanisms, hard verified-test rules, conflicts and own/opponent side attribution; those test rules are not claimed as real Champions verification.

The prerequisite sources are the pinned [Pokémon Showdown item definitions](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/3a766f2cfb5e51c1e428cfe87d0dba91a8f01640/data/items.ts) and its [Champions `canMegaEvo` implementation](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/3a766f2cfb5e51c1e428cfe87d0dba91a8f01640/data/mods/champions/scripts.ts). They are simulator sources, **not official Champions mechanic verification**. Their mapping was checked against the existing canonical item/form infrastructure. Four-move capacity is also a provisional simulator rule; the conservative projection leaves the remaining-slot count unknown without a verified capacity observation. The reference projection displays 2/2/3/3 unobserved slots for Kingambit/Sneasler/Salamence/Rillaboom, explicitly labeled as reference rules. More than a supported capacity produces a conflict without discarding revealed moves.

## Legality and eliminated possibilities

The existing KnowledgeBase is reused. Species-specific ability suggestions come from the versioned community catalog; available-form references come from existing identity records carrying Champions availability sources. Both remain **open/incomplete references**: unlisted abilities, forms, items and moves are not eliminated. Observations narrow move membership and historical possession without fabricating a complete legal build universe.

A finite legality domain becomes restrictive only when the caller supplies a complete, Champions-verified domain. Such domains can be labeled possible, intersected with evidence and pruned with traceable exclusions. No real recording candidate was eliminated merely for being absent from a database. The sample has zero hard or provisional constraint conflicts. Its unresolved cases include native ability provenance, unverified transformation requirements in default mode, incomplete legality references, unrevealed moves and all unobserved individual parameters.

## Remaining limits

This is deterministic evidence tracking, not an automatic proof of an opponent's entire original build. Native versus acquired ability/move origin can remain unresolved. General transfer/copy lifecycles, simultaneous multi-instance disjunction solving, exhaustive game-specific legality, battle-start item reconstruction and validated training/stat models are not supplied by this milestone. The relationship mechanism handles explicit implications and disjunction exclusions, not a general solver. Complex evidence remains visible for later modeling rather than being guessed.

The observed move-membership ledger assumes an ordinary configured execution unless an external/copy origin or relevant transformation is explicitly identified; it does not claim detection of unobserved move-copy mechanisms. No absent animation is used as proof of an absent ability/item. Provisional Mega prerequisites cannot establish official Champions rules. The conservative output intentionally contains fewer exact conclusions than the opt-in reference output.

No M2.3.2 Bayesian distributions, usage priors, M3 calculations, move prediction, recommendations, OCR training or unrelated tracker redesign was implemented. No commits or pushes were made.
