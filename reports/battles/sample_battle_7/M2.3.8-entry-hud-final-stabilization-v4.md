## Generic identity contract

Message-backed occupancy uses the existing Pokémon instances and active-entry episodes. `tracking/entry_hud.py` adds an episode-local audit, not another identity database. Every message-established episode tracks its first candidate HUD and first eligible independent icon/name observation. Unknown species require two compatible independent source frames; repeated reads or retained identity references supply no additional proof. Failed or ambiguous candidates stay visible in the audit, including failed reused observations. Contradictory icons break local corroboration. Superseded/departed episodes cannot supply proofs for another arrival.

The fixed entry association horizon is **30 seconds after arrival**, reusing the maintained named-message evidence horizon; corroboration must occur within **three seconds of the first eligible HUD**. These are conservative policy bounds, not measured recognition calibration. They do not depend on a nickname, species or recording timestamp. Expiry is recorded at its policy deadline on the next processed observation. A fresh late pair can establish current identity only with independent current-episode continuity; it is labelled `resolved_prospectively`, preserving the earlier expiry. Proof search is restricted to the recent three-second interval, never an unbounded accumulation of matching frames.

The audit distinguishes arrival, first candidate, first eligible proof, chosen identity source and final corroboration, resolution, expiry, and persistent-party identity. Resolution upgrades the original provisional instance at the actual evidence time. Prefix snapshots remain species-unknown before resolution. Canonical species/mechanics are never guessed from a nickname. Persistent party identity remains unproven unless supported separately. W scheduling and conservative correction policy remain auxiliary and once per command phase.

An overlay label alone does not describe every visible region: the saved 133.022s move-selection image still contains the genuine upper opponent battle HUD. Its name, HP bar and 2D icon region qualify for identity evidence; the screen does not qualify as a normal W command screen. Party/status/move-information regions cannot qualify as battle HUDs. Explicit non-HUD region metadata, failed names, reused identities, unresolved/conflicting icons and reliable-preview exclusions abstain.
