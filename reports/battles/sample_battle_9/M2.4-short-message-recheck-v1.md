## Method and durable receipts

Used the exact five image paths and SHA-256 values from the original `withdrawal-geometry.json`; all hashes matched. No video decoding, frame extraction, full acquisition or StateEngine replay was performed. Original sealed artifacts were preserved. No production source changes were made during this recheck.

Each frame was checked with both the previous and current geometry thresholds, followed by current `fast_profile.inspect()` and actual `read_message()` row OCR. Each frame received an independent row-read gate to measure its own OCR. A separate chronological message-region gate using `FastConfig.region_difference` and `MessageOccurrences(source_occurrence_ids=True)` checked the emitted events and deduplication within the cited sequence. It starts at the first cited frame of each battle, without prior full-run context.

## Acceptance boundary

This confirms visibility admission, automatic row OCR and withdrawal-event generation for these cited positive examples, including deduplication across the four Battle 9 samples. It does not establish the false-admission rate after globally lowering thresholds, broader recognition coverage, full-run scheduling behavior, or repair of later slot placement/binding. The original reports and histories still describe their original runs. No corrective full-state replay or milestone advancement was performed.
